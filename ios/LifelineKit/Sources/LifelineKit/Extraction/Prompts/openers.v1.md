# Opening questions, version v1
# One per line: id: text. Scenes, not facts. Never a date, a name list or a form question.
# Hard chapters are offered once and lightly. Edit freely; ids are what the log remembers.
house: Tell me about the house you grew up in.
meal: A meal you can still taste.
teacher: A teacher you remember.
first-place: The first place that felt like yours.
lost: A time you got lost.
kitchen: Who was usually in the kitchen, and what were they making?
summer: Tell me about one summer that stands out.
friend: Your first real friend. How did you meet?
job: Your first day at a job.
road: A long drive, and who was in the car.
pet: An animal that was part of the family.
music: A song that takes you straight back somewhere.
holiday: A holiday that did not go to plan.
neighbor: A neighbor everyone knew.
bike: Learning to ride, swim, or drive. Who taught you?
room: Describe your bedroom at ten years old.
storm: Weather you remember: a storm, a snow day, a heat wave.
game: A game you played until it got dark.
present: A present you never forgot.
smell: A smell that means home.
fight: A fight with a sibling or a friend that you can laugh about now.
move: The day you moved somewhere new.
grandparent: A visit to a grandparent's house.
school-lunch: What was in your lunch, and who did you sit with?
birthday: A birthday you remember, good or bad.
window: What could you see from the window where you did your homework?
lie: A small lie you told as a kid, and whether you got away with it.
hero: Someone you looked up to when you were young.
brave: A time you were braver than you felt.
secret-place: A hiding place or a secret spot.
dinner-table: What dinner was like at your table.
trouble: A time you got in trouble and the grown-up who dealt with it.
street: Walk me down your street as it was then.
photo: A photograph you wish you still had.
hospital: A time someone you loved was unwell, if you want to tell it.
leaving: Leaving home for the first time.
return: Going back to a place after years away.
laugh: The hardest you ever laughed with family.
sport: A team, a coach, a season.
chore: A chore you hated, and who made you do it.
