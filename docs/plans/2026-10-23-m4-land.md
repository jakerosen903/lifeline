# M4: Land — Implementation Plan (revised for the 2026-10-22 design review)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Every session lands lightly, however short: finishing never waits for sorting, the landing says the recording is saved and plays a few moments back, "Done for now" is the default, correction is one tap on any card, a heavy moment lands the session with a fixed response, and a crash resumes to the landing.

**Architecture:** A pure `SessionArc` holds the stages (`open → windDown → ended`), the session kind (quick story or settle in) and the wrap-up offer at the planned length; there is no automatic stop. `SessionRecorder.stop` persists and returns at once; the extraction queue finishes in the background and a launch-time `SortingWorker` re-enqueues anything left pending, so sorting is durable without blocking anyone. The session screen gains two views: Listen (default, quiet) and Transcript & memories (the review view with Keep / Change… / Dismiss and Undo). The landing (R6, R4 on the first session) shows saved moments with play. Visibility becomes two flags named by effect. Deleting today's recording removes the session's file and everything derived from it. Span-level deletion, stories, Share and Local-only are M5.

**Tech Stack:** Swift 6 strict concurrency, SwiftUI, Swift Testing, XCUITest, GRDB.swift 7.11.1, AVFoundation. No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-21-v1-listening-app-design.md` §15 (revisions) over §3, §7, §9, §11; `docs/DECISIONS.md` entries dated 2026-10-22 (preserve first; session length; correction; privacy controls; pipeline changes; readability; closing question) and 2026-10-23 (sessions short by default; daily activity direction); `docs/PRINCIPLES.md` P9 and the revised arc table; canvases `R1_FirstOpen`, `R2_Listen`, `R3_Review`, `R4_FirstPayoff`, `R6_Landing` (and `R5_Home`, `R7_Privacy` for copy only). Read the M1 to M3 plans for the code this extends.

## Global Constraints

- Everything in the M1 to M3 Global Constraints still holds, except where §15 changed it: the copy "nothing is saved until you say so" is removed everywhere; low confidence is a "Check this" label, never reduced opacity.
- Two session kinds, equally valid: `quick` (planned length 5 minutes) and `settleIn` (planned length 30 minutes), chosen at Start. `ArcConfig { quickPlanned, settleInPlanned, wrapUpGrace = 10 min, recordingLimit = 3 h }`, never literals in logic. At the planned length the app offers a wrap-up with an explicit "Keep going"; the only automatic stop is `recordingLimit`, a real file limit.
- Stages `open → windDown → ended`. Every change appends `{ "stage", "at", "reason" }` to `session.stage_log`. Reasons: `started`, `plannedLength`, `keepGoing`, `finish`, `wantsToStop`, `disclosure`, `recordingLimit`, `crash`, `landed`. The spec's `middle` stage is dropped (decision 2026-10-23).
- Finishing a session must not wait for extraction. `stop()` stops capture, writes `ended_at`, returns. Sorting continues in the background and is resumed at the next launch for any segment still pending. Nothing on the landing blocks on it; it shows "Still sorting: N more".
- The landing (R6): "Your recording is saved." · "A few moments from today." · "Sorting them is still going on in the background. You don't have to do anything now." · the moments with play · "Still sorting: N more" · "Before you go" with the closing line · "Done for now" (default) · "Review these moments" · "Hide today from the timeline" · "Delete today's recording…" (asks first). After a heavy session: "If you ever want to talk to a person, I can show you who to call." The first session ever shows the R4 variant: "Your first story is saved. Hear it back." with one moment and "Tell another".
- The closing line is neutral after a heavy session: "Thank you. That's enough for today." Never a question about the loss just discussed, never "something lighter". After a light session it is the top open question, phrased as in M3's opener: "Next time, if you like: <text>."
- Serious disclosures: the extractor's flag distinguishes narration of the past from present danger (`flags.disclosure: none | past | present`, extractor v3). Both land the session gently and suppress every organizational prompt for the rest of it, including "Who's this?". The fixed card (`Arc/FixedResponses/disclosure.en-US.md`) is shown verbatim: "Thank you for telling me that. You can stop here, or keep going. It is your choice. If you need to talk to someone now, call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, US)." For `present` the crisis line is also the first line of the landing. The model never writes any of it.
- Correction: every memory card offers Keep · Change… · Dismiss. Dismiss shows "Suggestion dismissed. Your recording is untouched." with Undo for 6 seconds, and never deletes recording content. Change… offers The title · Who was there · When it was; "Which part of the recording" and "Just tell me" are listed but disabled with "later" until M5 and the interviewer. Keeping a memory does not settle its inferred details: an inferred range shows "Check this: <range>?" and an unresolved name shows "Check this: <name>?" until settled.
- Visibility: `memory.hidden_from_timeline` and `memory.excluded_from_ai` replace the single `visibility`. Hidden memories leave everyday browsing but stay in Sessions; excluded ones never enter a context packet again. "Hide today from the timeline" sets the first flag on every memory of the session.
- "Delete today's recording…" removes the session's audio file, its segments, words, candidates, mentions, memories and edges (cascade) after a confirmation that names what goes. Span-level deletion with tombstones is M5.
- Readability: `FlowChips` wraps; ochre is always paired with a word or symbol; every swipe or long-press has a visible button alternative ("…" on memory rows).
- Pause: the Listen view has Pause / Resume. Pausing stops capture and the file clock stays continuous (the next chunk follows the last); the transcriber is told to finalize at pause.

## Review Focus

1. **The app is killed mid-session with sorting half done.** Next launch opens the landing for that session with the moments that exist, "Still sorting" counts the rest, and the worker finishes them without a tap. Task 2 (`launchWorkerReenqueuesPendingSegments`), Task 7 (UI `resumeAfterKillOpensTheLanding`).
2. **Finish is tapped while a request is in flight.** The landing appears at once; the candidate arrives a few seconds later and the landing's list and count update in place. Task 2 (`stopReturnsBeforeExtractionFinishes`), Task 6 (`landingUpdatesWhenSortingFinishes`).
3. **Dismiss then Undo within the window, and after it.** Undo restores the card exactly; after the window the suggestion is gone from the review view but comes back from Sessions → Dismissed (M3). Task 4 (`dismissUndoRestoresTheCard`, UI `dismissShowsUndo`).
4. **A disclosure flag on a segment whose cards were already shown.** The cards stay but no new organizational prompt appears, the fixed card shows once, and Finish lands to the neutral line. Task 1 (`disclosureSuppressesOrganizationalPrompts`), Task 5 (UI `disclosureCardShowsVerbatimAndLandsGently`).
5. **Delete today's recording while the worker is sorting it.** The worker's pending work for that session is dropped, the file goes, no row survives, and the timeline has nothing from it. Task 3 (`deleteSessionDropsPendingSortingAndEverything`).

### Task 1: SessionArc, fixed responses, disclosure kinds

**Files:** Create `Arc/ArcConfig.swift`, `Arc/SessionArc.swift`, `Arc/FixedResponses.swift`, `Arc/FixedResponses/disclosure.en-US.md`, `Extraction/Prompts/extractor.v3.md`; Modify `Package.swift` (resource `Arc/FixedResponses`), `ExtractorOutput.swift` (`Flags.disclosure: DisclosureKind`, decoding `serious_disclosure: true` from v2 rows as `.past`), `ExtractorSchema.swift`, `ExtractorConfig.swift` (version v3), `CardQueue.swift` (`heavy` suppresses memory **and** who-is-this cards), `ExtractionQueue.swift` (withholds memory rows as before; person rows too while heavy). **Interfaces (Produces):**
- `enum SessionKind: String, Codable { quick, settleIn }`; `struct ArcConfig { quickPlanned: Duration = .seconds(5*60), settleInPlanned = .seconds(30*60), wrapUpGrace = .seconds(10*60), recordingLimit = .seconds(3*3600); static let default; static let uiTesting (planned 6 s / 10 s, grace 4 s, limit 30 s) ; func planned(for: SessionKind) -> Duration }`.
- `enum Stage: String, Codable { open, windDown, ended }`; `enum ArcSignal: Equatable { tick(elapsed: Duration), keepGoing, finish, flags(Flags), recordingLimit, crash, landed }`; `enum ArcEffect: Equatable { offerWrapUp, showDisclosureCard, land }`.
- `struct SessionArc { init(kind:, config:, startedAt:); stage; log: [StageChange]; heavy: Bool; wrapUpOffered: Bool; mutating func apply(_:now:) -> ArcEffect? }`. Rules: `tick` past planned (and not yet offered) → `windDown`, `.offerWrapUp`; `.keepGoing` → stays `windDown`, logs, offers again after `wrapUpGrace`; `tick` past `recordingLimit`, `.finish`, `.recordingLimit` → `ended`, `.land`; `flags.wantsToStop` → `windDown` + `.offerWrapUp`, `heavy = true`; `flags.disclosure != .none` → `windDown`, `heavy = true`, `.showDisclosureCard` (once per session); after `ended` everything is ignored.
- `enum DisclosureKind: String, Codable { none, past, present }`; `Flags.disclosure`; `Flags.isHeavy` (`wantsToStop || disclosure != .none`); `Flags.isAnySet` unchanged in meaning.
- `FixedResponses.disclosure(locale:) throws -> String`, `crisisLine(locale:) -> CrisisLine { label, number }` (en-US only, fallback).
- Prompt v3 = v2 plus the `disclosure` field rule: "`past` when the person narrates harm that happened; `present` when they describe danger now or intent to harm themselves or others. `none` otherwise."

### Task 8: Device checklist (owner runs this; it is M4's done-when)

1. First open on a fresh install: two ways in, context optional. Tell a quick story, Finish within a minute. The landing says the recording is saved, shows the moment with play before sorting is done, and "Still sorting" clears on its own. Done for now.
2. Open the review view mid-session. Keep one card, Change… the title of another, Dismiss a third and Undo it. Nothing you did touched the recording.
3. A card whose range is a guess shows "Check this: around …?". Tap it, answer, the tag goes.
4. Settle in and talk past the planned length. The wrap-up appears once; Keep going; it appears again after the grace; it never stops on its own.
5. Pause mid-story, wait, Resume. The moment plays back without a gap or a jump.
6. Kill the app mid-session. Relaunch: the landing opens with what was captured; the rest sorts itself.
7. Say a sentence the extractor flags as past harm. The fixed card appears with 988; no "Who's this?" after it; Finish lands with the neutral line.
8. On a landing, Hide today. The timeline no longer shows it; Sessions still does; a later session's cards do not reference it once Exclude from AI is set from the "…" menu.
9. On a landing, Delete today's recording. Confirm. The file is gone (Sessions shows nothing), the timeline shows nothing from it, Console usage shows no later request about it.
10. Airplane mode, Finish. The landing opens at once with "Still sorting"; back online, relaunch: the moments appear.
