# M4: Land — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Every session lands, however short: on "Let's leave it there", on a wants-to-stop or serious-disclosure flag, after a crash, or, as a quiet backstop, after an hour, the owner sees what was heard in their own words and decides keep, private or let go for each memory, where let go really deletes the audio span, the words and the quotes.

**Architecture:** A pure `SessionArc` holds the stages and the stage log; the session view model drives it from a clock and from extraction flags. A `FixedResponses` loader serves the disclosure card verbatim from a bundled resource with a locale-keyed crisis line. `LetGo` does deletion in two parts: store rows in one transaction with a `tombstone`, and the audio span cut by an `AudioSpanCutter` (AVFoundation composition export) that can retry at launch if a file was not cuttable yet; playback maps stored offsets through tombstones with a pure `AudioOffsetMap`. A `WindDownScreen` shows the summary and the choices; on launch, an un-landed session with segments opens straight to it.

**Tech Stack:** Swift 6 strict concurrency, SwiftUI, Swift Testing, XCUITest, GRDB.swift 7.11.1, AVFoundation (`AVMutableComposition`, `AVAssetExportSession`). No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-21-v1-listening-app-design.md` sections 3 (Landing), 5 (Deletion), 6 (`tombstone`, private memories, invariants), 7 (flags), 9 (all of it), 11 row M4, 14 (disclosure, decided in `docs/DECISIONS.md` 2026-10-21). Canvases `docs/design/WindDown.dc.html` and `Onboard3.dc.html`. Read the M1 to M3 plans for the code this extends.

## Global Constraints

- Everything in the M1 to M3 Global Constraints still holds.
- Sessions are usually a few minutes long (owner, 2026-10-23). The button is how a session ends; the timer is a backstop, not a feature. `hardStopAt` default 60 minutes opens the summary by itself; `windDownAt` default 55 minutes shows a one-line nudge ("Still here? Leave it there whenever you like.") and nothing else. Both are fields of `ArcConfig`, never literals in logic. UI tests use a shortened config through `-uiTesting`. No length is ever shown as a goal or a progress bar.
- Stages `open → windDown → ended`; every change appends `{ "stage", "at", "reason" }` to `session.stage_log` (JSON array). Reasons: `started`, `timer`, `button`, `wantsToStop`, `seriousDisclosure`, `hardStop`, `crash`, `landed`. The spec's `middle` stage is dropped as bookkeeping with no effect (builder's call, logged in `docs/DECISIONS.md`).
- The disclosure card is a resource `Arc/FixedResponses/disclosure.en-US.md`; its text is exactly: "Thank you for telling me that. You can stop here, or keep going. It is your choice. If you need to talk to someone now, call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, US)." A unit test asserts the loader returns it byte for byte. The model never writes it. No other locale ships in V1; an unknown locale falls back to en-US.
- A session must not end without the wind-down screen, except on a crash; on next launch an un-landed session with at least one segment resumes straight to its wind-down with whatever was captured. One with no segments is closed silently.
- Wind-down: the list holds this session's accepted memories and pending memory candidates, each quoting the owner. Default is keep. Keep on a pending candidate accepts it. Private sets `visibility = private`. Let go on a pending candidate dismisses it and deletes its span. Let go on an accepted memory deletes every span of the memory, then the memory, then the audio.
- Let go means gone: the audio span is cut from the file, the word rows in the span are deleted, the segment's text is rebuilt from the words that remain (the segment row goes when none remain), candidate rows whose quote lies inside the span are deleted, a `tombstone` row (session_id, start_ms, end_ms, removed_at) records that a span was removed with no content. Stored offsets stay as recorded; playback maps them through tombstones.
- Private memories are excluded from the context packet, from the wind-down's closing line, and from the timeline unless its Private filter is on.
- The closing line is built from the top open curiosity item, or is a neutral line when the session carried a `seriousDisclosure` or `wantsToStop` signal: "Thank you. That's enough for today." The summary's subtitle is "Here's what I heard, in your words — nothing is saved until you say so." for every session; "That was a lot to carry." is added only when `heavy`. Copy from the canvases: "Let's leave it there for today.", "Keep them on my timeline", "Keep them, but just for me", "Let them go", "The recording is deleted too", "Before you go — something lighter", "Or skip it. Either is fine.", "Done for today", "If you ever want to talk to a person, I can show you who to call."
- `wants_to_change_subject` lands nothing; in V1 it only logs, since no curiosity card is shown mid-session yet.

## Review Focus

1. **The app is killed during a session, before any card was acted on.** Next launch opens the wind-down for that session with its memories and pending cards; nothing is lost and nothing is auto-kept. Task 7 (UI `resumeAfterKillOpensWindDown` using `app.terminate()` and relaunch).
2. **Let go on a memory whose span is the whole segment, in a file still being written.** Rows go at once; the audio cut is queued and completes on the next launch; playback of neighbouring segments still lands on the right words. Task 3 (`letGoRemovesTheWholeSegmentAndQueuesTheCut`), Task 4 (`queuedCutCompletesOnRetry`), Task 3 (`offsetsMapThroughTombstones`).
3. **A serious disclosure arrives after the wind-down already started.** The fixed card still shows, nothing improvised appears, the stage log records it once. Task 1 (`disclosureDuringWindDownIsLoggedOnce`), Task 6 (`disclosureCardShowsVerbatim`).
4. **Hard stop while the owner is mid-sentence.** Capture stops when the summary opens, the last partial words are stored as a segment (M1 rule), and extraction drains within its deadline. Task 6 (`hardStopStopsCaptureAndOpensTheSummary`).
5. **The owner backgrounds the app on the wind-down and comes back a day later.** The screen is still there with the same choices; nothing was decided for them. Task 7 (`windDownSurvivesBackgrounding` with `XCUIDevice.shared.press(.home)` and reactivate).

### Task 1: SessionArc

**Files:** Create `Arc/ArcConfig.swift`, `Arc/SessionArc.swift`; Test `SessionArcTests.swift`.

**Interfaces (Produces):**
- `struct ArcConfig: Sendable { windDownAt: Duration = .seconds(55 * 60), hardStopAt: Duration = .seconds(60 * 60); static let default; static let uiTesting = ArcConfig(windDownAt: .seconds(6), hardStopAt: .seconds(12)) }`.
- `enum Stage: String, Codable { open, windDown, ended }`, `enum ArcSignal: Equatable { tick(elapsed: Duration), button, flags(Flags), hardStop, crash, landed }`, `struct StageChange: Codable, Equatable { stage: Stage, at: Date, reason: String }`.
- `struct SessionArc: Equatable, Sendable { init(config:, startedAt:); private(set) var stage; private(set) var log: [StageChange]; var heavy: Bool (a seriousDisclosure or wantsToStop was seen); mutating func apply(_ signal: ArcSignal, now: Date) -> ArcEffect? }` with `enum ArcEffect: Equatable { showWindDownBanner, showDisclosureCard, openSummary }`. Rules: `tick` past `windDownAt` → `windDown` with `.showWindDownBanner` (the one-line nudge); `tick` past `hardStopAt`, `.button`, `.hardStop` → `ended` with `.openSummary`; `flags.wantsToStop` → `windDown` and `.showWindDownBanner`; `flags.seriousDisclosure` → `windDown` and `.showDisclosureCard` (and `heavy = true`), a second disclosure in `windDown` logs once more but returns `.showDisclosureCard` again (the card is idempotent on screen); `flags.wantsToChangeSubject` → log only, nil; signals after `ended` are ignored. `log` encodes to the `stage_log` JSON (`static func encode/decode`).

- [ ] Tests: `startsOpenWithOneLogEntry`, `aShortSessionEndsByTheButtonWithNoTimerEntries` (a 3-minute session: log is started, button), `timerMovesToWindDownOnceAndShowsTheBanner`, `buttonEndsFromAnyStageAndOpensTheSummary`, `wantsToStopWindsDown`, `seriousDisclosureWindsDownAndShowsTheCard`, `disclosureDuringWindDownIsLoggedOnce`, `hardStopEndsEvenMidWindDown`, `signalsAfterEndedAreIgnored`, `logRoundTripsThroughJSON`. ### Task 3: Tombstones, offset map, and the store side of let go

**Files:** Create `Deletion/AudioOffsetMap.swift`, `Deletion/LetGo.swift`, `Store/TombstoneRepository.swift`; Modify `Store/Migrations.swift` (v5: `tombstone` per spec plus `pending_cut` (tombstone_id, attempts, last_error)), `Store/AppDatabase.swift`, `Store/SessionRepository.swift` (`rebuildSegmentText`, `deleteWords(in:ms:)`, `deleteSegment`), `Store/CandidateRepository.swift` (`deleteQuoted(within:)`), `Store/MemoryRepository.swift` (`delete` exists). **Interfaces (Produces):**
- `struct Tombstone: Identifiable { id: TombstoneID, sessionID, startMs, endMs, removedAt: Date }`, `TombstoneRepository.insert/tombstones(in:)/pendingCuts()/markCut(_:)/recordCutFailure(_:error:)`.
- `enum AudioOffsetMap { static func fileMs(forStored ms: Milliseconds, tombstones: [Tombstone]) -> Milliseconds? }` returns nil when the stored point lies inside a removed span, else `ms − sum(length of tombstones that end at or before ms)`; `static func fileRange(forStored range: MsRange, tombstones:) -> MsRange?`.
- `struct LetGo: Sendable { init(store: ExtractionStore, tombstones: TombstoneRepository); func memory(_ id: MemoryID) throws -> [Tombstone]; func candidate(_ candidate: Candidate) throws -> [Tombstone] }`. One transaction per span: tombstone row, `pending_cut` row, word rows in the span deleted, segment text rebuilt or segment deleted, candidate rows whose quote (as word span) lies inside deleted (the memory's own candidate included), memory and its edges deleted (memory), or candidate deleted (pending). Returns the tombstones so the caller can cut audio.

### Task 4: Cutting the audio

**Files:** Create `Deletion/AudioSpanCutter.swift`; Test `AudioSpanCutterTests.swift` (uses `AudioFileWriter` to synthesize a 6 s tone file, as M1's tests do).

**Interfaces (Produces):** `actor AudioSpanCutter { init(tombstones: TombstoneRepository, audioURL: @Sendable (SessionID) throws -> URL); func cut(_ tombstone: Tombstone) async throws; func retryPending() async -> Int }`. `cut` builds an `AVMutableComposition` from the asset's audio track with every range except `[start, end)` of the file (after mapping through earlier tombstones of the same session, oldest first), exports with `AVAssetExportSession` preset `AVAssetExportPresetAppleM4A` to a temporary file, replaces the original atomically, then `markCut`. On any error it `recordCutFailure` and rethrows; `retryPending` tries every pending cut once and returns how many succeeded.

### Task 8: Device checklist (owner runs this; it is M4's done-when)

1. Record for a minute, tap "Let's leave it there". The summary lists the memories with Keep selected and no mention of time. Done. The timeline shows them.
2. Once, when convenient: leave a session running with the screen off past an hour. At 55 minutes a one-line nudge appears; at 60 the summary opens on its own and recording has stopped.
3. On a summary, choose Let go for one memory. Done. Play the neighbouring moment: it still plays the right words. The let-go words are not in the session detail, not in People's story, not in the timeline.
4. Choose Just for me for one memory. It is gone from the timeline until you turn on Private; it never appears in a later session's cards as context.
5. Say "I don't want to talk about this today". The banner appears; the summary's closing line is the neutral one.
6. Say a sentence the extractor would flag (you decide the words). The fixed card appears with 988; no memory card follows; the summary carries the crisis line.
7. Kill the app mid-session. Relaunch: the wind-down opens with what was captured.
8. Background the app on the wind-down for an hour, come back: same screen, same choices.
9. Let go of a memory while the session's file was still being written (kill, relaunch, let go). The words go at once; the next launch prints that the queued cut completed; the file is shorter.
10. Check the Console usage page: private memories never travel in a packet after being marked.
