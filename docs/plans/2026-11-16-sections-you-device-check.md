Use the owner's iPhone with the owner's real archive. Tick each line when it is true.

- [ ] 1. You reads as a portrait in light and dark appearance: name, birth line, strip, lead line, then the parts. No part shows empty. (There is no dark palette yet, so dark may fail.)
