# M3: Place — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Memories get time ranges from what the owner said, the timeline shows them as honest bands inside chapters and collections with a shelf for the unplaced, one relative question at a time narrows a range, and the app knows which question would place the most memories.

**Architecture:** A pure `Resolver` in a new `Resolution` module turns anchors plus a birth date plus already-placed targets into `TimeRange`s, floating memories and curiosity items; it is a function, so the CLI and the app get the same answer and a full re-run equals the incremental runs. A `ResolverRunner` applies its output to the store after each accepted memory and on demand. `RelativeQuestion` (pure) builds the one question a range admits from things the owner said; answering sets a confirmed range. The timeline replaces the session list as the home screen; sessions move behind a toolbar link. Chapters and collections come from accepting the extractor's hint cards or from a long-press on a memory.

**Tech Stack:** Swift 6 strict concurrency, SwiftUI, Swift Testing, XCUITest, GRDB.swift 7.11.1. No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-21-v1-listening-app-design.md` sections 3 (Timeline, Memory), 6 (time range, chapter, collection, curiosity_item, invariants), 7 (collections and chapters), 8 (Resolution, all of it), 9 (pending cards on the timeline), 10 (`resolve --all`), 11 row M3. Design canvases: `docs/design/Timeline.dc.html`, `FuzzyA.dc.html`, `FuzzyB.dc.html`, `FuzzyC.dc.html`, `Event.dc.html`, `Refine.dc.html` and the decision in `docs/design/README.md`: data model B (every memory has a range plus optional collection), default view A (collections), C's shelf for memories with neither date nor collection. The M1 and M2 plans describe the code this extends. Read them.

## Global Constraints

- Everything in the M1 and M2 Global Constraints still holds (iOS 26, Swift 6 strict, app target holds only views, view models and the composition root; all logic in `LifelineKit`; fixtures invented; never push).
- Time range columns on `memory` and `chapter`: `earliest`, `latest`, `likely_start`, `likely_end` as `YYYY-MM-DD` text or null; `granularity` in `day, month, season, year, decade, era`; `origin` in `stated, inferred, derived, confirmed`; `evidence_text` is the owner's phrase. A memory with all four dates null is unplaced.
- The resolver must never ask for a date. It may generate one relative question per anchor, phrased as a choice between things the owner said. There is no date picker anywhere in M3.
- A confirmed range (`origin = confirmed`) is never overwritten by the resolver.
- Re-running the resolver over all memories must produce the same result as incremental runs: `Resolver.resolve` is a pure function of anchors, birth date, chapter ranges and confirmed ranges.
- `origin = inferred` renders as a guess in every view: the ochre "around", "probably", "sometime in" wording from the canvases, never a bare date.
- Nothing in `chapter.title`, `collection.title` or `curiosity_item.text` may contain text the owner did not say or type, except that a chapter or collection created from a hint card takes the hint phrase, which is the owner's words by the extractor's rules.
- Relation span caps (builder's values, configuration in `ResolverConfig`): `before`/`after` widen by at most 5 years from the target's edge; `during` and `same_year_as` copy the target's range; an `age` anchor gives the one-year band from the birthday; a grade phrase gives the school year (US: grade N runs from 1 September of birth year + N + 5 to 30 June of the year after).
- Copy fixed by the canvases: "Your story so far", "Waiting for you", "memories waiting for a place" / "1 memory waiting for a place", "Pin it", "Keep telling your story", "Narrow it down", "Best guess so far", "Keep “<range>” for now", "You can always come back to this.", "Somewhere in <chapter>", "no group yet", "Sounds like it belongs here", "In this memory", "In your voice".

## Review Focus

Each line names an input the spec implies but no acceptance criterion exercises, and the task whose tests pin it.

1. **No birth date, then one arrives.** Age anchors float until the owner adds a birthday in Settings; the next resolver run places them without touching anything confirmed. Task 3 (`ageAnchorFloatsWithoutBirthDate`), Task 4 (`addingABirthDateRunsTheResolverAndPlacesAgeMemories`).
2. **Two anchors disagree.** "In 1998" and "when I was six" on one memory with a 1986 birthday overlap nowhere. The memory keeps the stated range, the age anchor stays unresolved, and no curiosity item asks about it. Task 3 (`contradictoryAnchorsKeepTheStatedRange`).
3. **A relative chain with a cycle.** A before B, B before A. The resolver terminates, places neither from the other, and reports both as floating. Task 3 (`cyclicRelativeAnchorsTerminate`).
4. **Answering a relative question, then the target moves.** The owner says "before Dad fell in"; later Dad's memory is confirmed to 2001. The answered memory keeps its confirmed range; the resolver never widens or moves a confirmed range. Task 6 (`confirmedRangeSurvivesATargetChange`).
5. **The timeline with nothing placed.** First open after M2 with only unplaced memories: the timeline shows one shelf, no empty year axis, the "Waiting for you" cards, and the Start button still reachable. Task 7 (UI `timelineWithOnlyUnplacedMemoriesShowsTheShelf`).

### Task 3: The resolver

**Files:**
- Create: `Resolution/ResolverConfig.swift`, `Resolution/Resolver.swift`
- Test: `ResolverTests.swift`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `TimeRange`, `CivilDate`, `Anchor`, `AnchorPhrase`, `Chapter`.
- Produces:
  - `struct ResolverConfig: Sendable { relativeSlackYears = 5, ageBandYears = 1, schoolYearStartMonth = 9, schoolYearEndMonth = 6, gradeOffsetYears = 5; static let `default` }`.
  - `struct ResolverInput: Sendable { memories: [MemoryRef], anchors: [Anchor], birthDate: CivilDate?, chapters: [Chapter], config: ResolverConfig }` where `struct MemoryRef { id: MemoryID, title: String, range: TimeRange, primarySpan: (SegmentID, Milliseconds, Milliseconds) }`.
  - `struct ResolverOutput: Equatable, Sendable { placements: [MemoryID: TimeRange], floating: [FloatingTarget], curiosity: [CuriosityDraft], resolvedAnchorIDs: Set<AnchorID> }`, `struct FloatingTarget { phrase: String, anchorID: AnchorID, fromMemory: MemoryID }` (a target phrase no memory matches; the runner creates the floating memory), `struct CuriosityDraft { kind: CuriosityKind, text: String, payoffCount: Int, sourceMemoryID: MemoryID?, targetPhrase: String? , targetMemoryID: MemoryID? }`.
  - `enum Resolver { static func resolve(_ input: ResolverInput) -> ResolverOutput }`. Pure. Algorithm, per spec §8 in order, iterated to a fixed point (at most `memories.count + 1` passes, so a cycle terminates): 1 absolute → `stated`; 2 age with birth date → `inferred` (band from `AgeSpan`: `years(a, b)` gives birthday + a to birthday + b + 1 minus a day; `grade(n)` gives the school year; `approximate(c, s)` gives c − s to c + s + 1); 3 `before`/`after`/`during`/`same_year_as` with a resolved target (target = the memory whose title or evidence text matches `targetText` by the M2 word-token rule, or a chapter title) → `inferred` with the relation's band (after X: earliest = X.earliest, latest = X.latest + slack; before X: earliest = X.earliest − slack, latest = X.latest; during and same_year_as copy X); 4 relative to an unplaced or unknown target → `FloatingTarget` and a curiosity draft `unresolvedAnchor` with text = the target phrase and `payoffCount` = the number of distinct memories whose anchors point at that phrase; 5 is a view concern (Task 7). Several anchors on one memory intersect; when the intersection is empty the first stated range wins and the others are left unresolved. A memory whose stored range is `confirmed` is never in `placements`. `resolvedAnchorIDs` lists every anchor that contributed to a placement.

### Task 6: The relative question

**Files:**
- Create: `Resolution/RelativeQuestion.swift`
- Test: `RelativeQuestionTests.swift`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `struct RelativeQuestion: Equatable, Sendable { memoryID: MemoryID, prompt: String, choices: [Choice] }`, `struct Choice: Equatable, Identifiable { id: String, label: String, resultingRange: TimeRange }`.
  - `enum RelativeQuestions { static func question(for memory: MemoryRef, anchors: [Anchor], placed: [MemoryRef], birthDate: CivilDate?, config: ResolverConfig) -> RelativeQuestion? }`. Pure. Order of preference, one question only: (a) the memory has an unresolved relative anchor whose target is placed → "Was this before or after <target title>?" with choices Before / After and the resulting bands; (b) the memory is placed `inferred` with a band wider than one year and the collection or chapter holds a placed memory inside the band → "Was this the same <year> as <that memory>?" with Yes (copy its range) / No (band minus that year, split into the larger side); (c) the memory is unplaced and the owner has a birth date → "Was this before or after you turned ten?" is a date question in disguise and is **not** allowed; return nil. Every choice label is a memory title or the owner's phrase; never a bare date except inside parentheses after a title, as the Refine canvas shows "(2000)".
  - `static func apply(_ choice: Choice, to memory: MemoryRef) -> TimeRange` returns the choice's range with `origin = .confirmed` and `evidenceText = choice.label`.
  - `CandidateActions.answer(_ question: RelativeQuestion, with choice: Choice) throws` writes the range, marks the anchor resolved, closes the matching curiosity item, runs the resolver.

### Task 8: Device checklist (owner runs this; it is M3's done-when)

1. Open the app. The timeline shows your existing memories on the shelf under "Somewhere in your story", and "Waiting for you" holds any pending cards.
2. Settings, add your birthday, Done. Memories anchored by age ("when I was eleven") move onto a band with "around" wording and the age.
3. Tell a story with a year in it ("in 2004"). Accept the card. It appears as a stated band with the bare year.
4. Tell a story "the summer after I graduated". Accept. It goes to the shelf and the timeline asks about "graduated" with a payoff count.
5. Tell the graduation story with a year. Accept. The earlier memory moves next to it, inferred.
6. Tap a memory, tap its range, answer "before" or "after". The range tightens and reads as confirmed. Tell a conflicting story later; the confirmed range does not move.
7. Accept a "Sounds like it belongs here" hint. The collection shows with its derived span. Long-press a memory, make a new chapter; its range follows its members.
8. Play a memory from the memory screen. The span plays and the words match.
9. Copy the database off and run `swift run lifeline resolve --all --db <path>`; it prints no changes on a second run.
10. Kill the app during a session with an age anchor and relaunch; the next accept places it.
