# M7 — Elements: moments on the timeline, everything else by what it is about

Status: draft for the owner's review, 2026-10-24. Follows M6 (PR #9). Spec, not plan. The owner set the direction in conversation; calls the owner did not make are marked **(my call)** so they can be overturned while reading.

## Scope, and what this spec is not

The archive today has one element, the memory, and expects every one of them to have a date eventually. The owner's observation: much of what a person says is not a moment at all. It is a thing they had (a wall in their room, a first house, a first car), a place, a person, a thought or a perspective, or general information about their life. Forcing those onto a timeline makes the shelf fill with things that will never have a date and makes the app ask "when was this?" about things that never happened at a time.

M7 gives the archive four kinds of **entity** beside its **moments**, gives entities pages of their own, and makes the timeline one view among several. It does not add the spoken interviewer (M8), replay (M9) or photos (Phase 2). It does not reclassify anything without a tap.

## Why now

Before the interviewer can ask "tell me about the car" there has to be a car to point at. Before replay can walk "your places," places need pages and spans. And the owner is already seeing the misfit: undated facts on the shelf, a resolver question about a sentence that is not an event.

## Goals

1. **Facts leave the timeline today.** A memory the extractor marked `fact` (standing information, not an event) is never shown as waiting for a date, never asked "when?", and never placed by the resolver. It appears under "About" until Goal 2 gives it a proper home. This is the one-day step; it ships first.
2. **Four entities with pages.** People (exist), Places (exist, gain a page and a span), Things (new), Ideas (new). An entity is reached by what it is about: the People, Places, Things and Ideas screens, and from any moment that mentions it. Its page shows the entity itself first, described in the person's words and dated by its span, then the moments it appears in **(my call, the owner's open question)**.
3. **Standing statements attach to entities.** "The house had a blue door" is a detail of the Thing "the house"; "my grandmother was Portuguese" is a detail of the Person; "I believed hard work was everything" is an Idea. A statement about the person themself goes to **About you**, which grows out of the profile's free text.
4. **Ideas change over time.** An Idea keeps its moments in order, so "I used to think X" and "now I think Y" are one idea with two dated moments, not two unrelated memories.
5. **Domains as a second axis.** Home, family, work, health, travel, and whatever the person says, as free-text tags on any element, from hints the extractor hears. No fixed list in V1.
6. **Curiosity asks the right question.** "When?" only about moments. For entities: "Who else lived there?", "What happened to the car?", "Do you still think that?"

## Non-goals and anti-goals

- No taxonomy the person has to learn. Four entity words, in plain English, and the card says which it heard.
- Nothing is reclassified silently. The extractor proposes; the card shows; a tap confirms or changes; older rows keep their old shape until a newer extractor version backfills them and the person sees the result.
- No entity without a telling behind it. Every entity is created from something the person said, with a span pointing at the words, like a memory.
- A failure of M7 is a timeline that got emptier and a set of pages the person never opens. Entities must be one tap from the moments that mention them and back.

## Goal 1 — Facts leave the timeline (the one-day step)

- **Timeline.** A memory whose card kind is `fact` is left out of the shelf and the chapters. A new section "About" lists them (title, the quote). The summary line counts them as "and n things about your life".
- **Resolver and curiosity.** A `fact` memory is never placed and never the subject of an `unresolved_anchor` question or a relative question. "Add a date" and "Tell me when" are not offered on it.
- **Cards.** The `fact` card already reads "Something about your life"; Keep lands it under About. Nothing else changes.
- **Data.** No migration: the kind is in the payload. **(my call)** This step is small enough to ship as its own PR before the rest of M7, so the owner feels the difference.

## Goal 2 — Entities

**Model.** One table `entity (id, type: person | place | thing | idea, title, detail_text, created_from_candidate_id, created_at, hidden_from_timeline, excluded_from_ai)` and one link table `moment_entity (memory_id, entity_id, role_text)`. `person` and `place` rows migrate into it: the `person` table stays as the owner of aliases, birth date and mentions, and gets an `entity_id`; `place` becomes an entity type **(my call: one page model for all four beats three parallel ones; the person table's extras stay where they are)**. An entity has `spans` like a memory (`entity_span`), so its first telling plays.

**Span of an entity.** Derived, never asked: the earliest and latest of its moments' ranges, with the moments' uncertainty ("between 1993 and 1998" when the moments are guesses). A Thing or Place may also carry a *stated* span from "Tell me when" phrased as "From when to when did you have it?" **(my call)**.

**Pages.** Each entity has a screen: the title, the detail in the person's words (the standing statements, Goal 3), the span, Play of its first telling, the moments it appears in (as timeline rows), and the people, places and things that co-occur. Entry points: the People screen (exists), new Places, Things and Ideas screens from the timeline toolbar, and tapping an entity chip on a memory. **(my call)** The four screens are one screen with a segmented control, not four tabs.

**Cards.** The extractor's mention types grow from `person | place` to `person | place | thing | idea`. A new "Sounds like a thing you had" / "Sounds like a place" / "Sounds like something you think" card follows the who's-this card's shape: the phrase, Keep / Not quite, and for a thing or place a span of the words that describe it. A card that names an entity already on file links instead of creating.

**Resolving a mention** already links every memory in the segment that named the person; the same holds for things, places and ideas.

## Goal 3 — Standing statements attach to entities

The extractor's `fact` kind gains an optional `about_ref`: the entity (from the packet) or the mention (from this output) the statement is about, or `self` for the person. Keep writes the memory with its span as today and links it to the entity as a *detail*; the entity's page shows details first, moments after. A fact with no `about_ref` goes to About you. The person can move a detail between entities from the memory's Change sheet ("About whom or what?").

## Goal 4 — Ideas change over time

An Idea's page orders its moments by date and shows each moment's quote as one line, so the change reads top to bottom. The extractor is told: a thought or belief the person states about themselves, the world, or how to live is an `idea` mention; a moment where they say it changed is a moment linked to the same idea. No sentiment analysis, no summary of what the person "really" thinks.

## Goal 5 — Domains

A `domain_tag (element_kind, element_id, text)` table **(my call)**. The extractor's `domain_hint` on a memory or mention yields a hint card like the collection hint ("Sounds like it belongs to: home"). Tags filter the timeline and the entity screens. Nothing is tagged without a tap.

## Goal 6 — Curiosity

New curiosity kinds: `entity_without_span` (a place or thing with no dated moment), `person_without_relation`, `idea_without_change`. Each has one plain question. "When?" questions keep their rule: moments only.

## Packet and prompt

The context packet carries entities with their type and a one-line detail (budgeted like memories, after people). Extractor prompt v6 adds the `thing` and `idea` mention kinds, `about_ref`, `domain_hint`, and the rule: "A thing, place or idea named in the segment is a mention, not a memory. A sentence that describes it is a fact about it."

## Copy

- Cards: "Sounds like a thing you had", "Sounds like a place", "Sounds like something you think", "Something about your life" (exists).
- Timeline section: "About" (Goal 1), later "About you".
- Entity screens: "People", "Places", "Things", "Ideas"; an entity page's span: "from 1993 to 1998" / "around 1998" / "no dates yet"; the Tell-me-when variant: "From when to when?"
- Curiosity: "Where was this?" stays; new "Who else was there?", "What became of {thing}?", "Do you still think that?"

## Migration

v9: `entity`, `entity_span`, `moment_entity`, `domain_tag`; `person.entity_id`; `place` rows copied into `entity` with `type = place` and `memory_place` into `moment_entity`; `place` and `memory_place` kept one release and read nowhere **(my call: drop in v10)**. Older `fact` payloads have no `about_ref`; they land in About until a re-run or the person moves them.

## Tests the plan must carry

- Kit: `fact` memories excluded from shelf rows, resolver placement and relative questions; About section rows; entity CRUD and span derivation with uncertainty; migration v9 on a populated v8 database (places become entities, links kept); mention resolution links things and ideas across the segment; `about_ref` lands a detail on its entity, `self` on About you, none on About; an idea's moments order by date; domain hint accepted tags the element; new curiosity kinds fire and stop; packet carries entities within budget; prompt v6 loads.
- UI: a scripted `fact` card kept lands under About and gets no "Add a date"; a scripted "thing" mention becomes a card, Keep opens its page with the moment under it; a place page shows its span; an idea with two scripted moments reads in order; the Things screen filters by a domain tag.

3. An entity page shows the thing itself first, its moments after.
