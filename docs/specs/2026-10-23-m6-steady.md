# M6 — Steady: see what is still sorting, keep a copy, trim a telling

Status: draft for the owner's review, 2026-10-23. Follows M5 (branch `m5-organize`, PR pending). Spec, not plan. The owner asked for a draft without the usual questions; calls the owner did not make are marked **(my call)** so they can be overturned while reading.

## Scope, and what this spec is not

M5 made the timeline organize itself with the person's consent. M6 makes daily use steady: the person can see that a recording is still being sorted, can keep a copy of everything and get it back on a new phone, and can trim a telling to the words that belong to it. It also tidies two test flakes that cost every run a retry.

Not in M6: the privacy screen, span-level deletion and the extractor re-run (out of V1, decision 2026-10-23); "Just tell me" on the Change sheet, which belongs to the spoken interviewer; iCloud or any server-side backup (the server keeps nothing, V1 data posture).

## Why now

After a recording the landing says "Sorting them is still going on in the background", and then the timeline says nothing. Cards appear in "Waiting for you" when they land; the duplicate pass that follows a Keep is silent too. On a slow network that looks like half the recording was lost (owner, 2026-10-23). The database and audio live only on the phone; one lost phone is the whole archive. The Change sheet shows two disabled rows that promise correction it cannot do yet.

## Goals

1. **Sorting is visible wherever the person looks.** The timeline shows one quiet line while anything from a recording is still being sorted, and says so when sorting stopped (no key, no network). The Sessions screen marks the recording that is behind.
2. **A copy the person owns.** Export everything to one file, restore it on any phone running the app. Nothing leaves the phone unless the person shares the file.
3. **Trim a telling.** "Which part of the recording" on the Change sheet lets the person move the start and end of a telling to the words that belong to it. Words are never deleted; the span moves.
4. **Honest tests.** The two kit tests that flake under a parallel run become deterministic.

## Non-goals and anti-goals

- No progress bars with percentages: sorting is a count of moments, not a fraction.
- No background upload, no account. A failure of M6 is a copy of the archive going anywhere the person did not send it.
- Trimming never edits words or audio; it changes where a telling starts and ends.

## Goal 1 — Sorting status

**What the person sees.** At the top of "Waiting for you" (or under the summary when there are no cards yet), one line with the landing's glyph:

- "Still sorting 2 moments from today's recording…" while segments of the newest session are pending; "from Tuesday's recording" when it is an older one; "…from 2 recordings" when several.
- "Checking for repeats…" while the duplicate pass is running. Pair cards can arrive a few seconds after a Keep.
- "Not sorting right now" when the last attempt failed on the network; "Add a key in Settings to sort what you say", tappable, when there is no key. The copy is the session screen's.
- Nothing at all at rest. The line never shows for a recording the person deleted or hid.

On the Sessions screen a session with pending segments carries the same line under its row.

**How it works (my call).** The kit gains `SortingStatus` (a value: pending segment counts by session, whether any queue or the worker is active, the last failure kind, whether the duplicate pass is running) and a pure `SortingStatus.line(now:)` for the copy. "Still sorting…" shows only while a queue or the worker is active; pending segments with nothing active read "Not sorting right now" with a "Try again" tap (or the key line when there is no key). `SortingWorker` and `ExtractionQueue` report activity and the last failure through a small `SortingReporter` the composition root owns; `DuplicatePass` reports running / idle through its `onFinished` hook plus an `onStarted`. The timeline polls the store every second only while the status is non-empty, the way the landing does; otherwise it reads once per refresh.

**Sorting runs again (my call).** Today the worker runs once at launch and stops after a no-key failure. M6 makes `SortingWorker.start()` re-entrant (a call while a pass runs is a no-op) and calls it again when a key is saved in Settings, when the app returns to the foreground with pending segments, and from the line's "Try again". Without this the tappable key line would promise nothing.

**Hidden recordings count (my call).** A recording hidden from the timeline still sorts and still lands cards, so the line counts it; only a deleted recording vanishes from it.

## Goal 2 — Backup and restore

**What the person sees.** Settings gains a "Your copy" section:

- "Export a copy…" builds one file, `Lifeline-2026-10-23.lifeline` (a zip), and opens the share sheet so the person can save it to Files, AirDrop it, or mail it to themselves. A line under the button says how big it is and when the last export was.
- "Restore from a copy…" opens the file picker, shows what is inside ("14 recordings, 31 memories, from 2026-10-23"), and asks once: "Replace everything on this phone with this copy?" Restore replaces; it does not merge **(my call)**. Before anything is replaced the app writes a safety copy of what is on the phone now, `Lifeline-before-restore-2026-10-23.lifeline`, into its Documents folder where Files can see it, and says so **(my call: "preserve first" means a restore can never be the move that loses yesterday's recordings; the person deletes the safety copy when they are sure)**. The copy is applied at the next launch: the app says "Your copy is ready. Close Lifeline and open it again to finish." **(my call: the data layer, the sorting worker and the duplicate pass all hold the open database; swapping it under them is not safe, and a staged restore applied before the app opens its storage is the one path that cannot tear)**.
- Export and restore are refused while a session is live or unlanded ("Finish your recording first"); pending segments do not block them, they are data and travel with the copy **(my call)**.
- A copy whose schema version is newer than the app's is refused with "This copy was made by a newer version of Lifeline."; the app version in the manifest is informational. A copy with an older schema is migrated on open, as a database is today **(my call: schema, not app version, so a TestFlight build one number ahead does not reject its own copy)**.

**How it works (my call).** The archive holds `lifeline.sqlite` (after a WAL checkpoint, copied through GRDB's backup API so a live write cannot tear it), the `audio/` directory, `pauses.log`, and `manifest.json` (app version, schema version, counts, created-at). The API key is never in the archive. Restore unpacks to a staging directory, checks the manifest, writes the safety copy, then leaves a "pending restore" marker; the next launch swaps the directories atomically before `Dependencies.live()` opens the database and re-applies the no-backup file attribute to `audio/`. `AudioLocation` already owns the paths. Export runs off the main actor with a progress sentence ("Packing 14 recordings…"); the share sheet opens when the file is ready. The last export time lives in UserDefaults so a restored phone does not inherit it. The `.lifeline` type is declared in `project.yml` for the picker.

**Zip library (owner's call).** Foundation on iOS has no public zip API. The options are ZIPFoundation (one small dependency added to `Package.swift`; the file opens in Files on a Mac), Apple's AppleArchive (`.aar`, no dependency, does not open in Finder as a folder), or a minimal store-only zip writer in the kit (no dependency, a few hundred lines to own). The draft assumes ZIPFoundation.

## Goal 3 — Which part of the recording

**What the person sees.** On the Change sheet, "Which part of the recording" opens a sheet with the telling's words laid out in order, the current span highlighted. Dragging the start or end handle, or tapping a word, moves the edge to a word boundary; the span may grow as well as shrink, within its segment **(my call: the extractor's span is a guess, and the person's words may run further than it chose)**. Play plays the new span. Save writes it; the memory's quote stays the extractor's words until the person edits the title. A telling told across a pause is edited one part at a time: a control at the top picks the part, each part is trimmed within its own segment, and no part can be removed here **(my call)**. Undo stays offered until the sheet closes, as the sheet's merge does **(my call)**.

"Just tell me" stays listed, disabled, with the same note as today **(my call)**: its shape should stay visible until the interviewer lands.

**How it works (my call).** `MemoryRepository.retime(span:startMs:endMs:)` updates one span's bounds within its segment (word times from `word`), one transaction; the resolver does not run, because time within a segment carries no date. `CandidateActions.trimTelling(_:order:startWord:endWord:)` validates the words, calls it, and rewrites the matching `segment_span` or `extra_spans` entry in the card's payload so a later Separate or Keep agrees with the trim. A merge receipt taken before the trim no longer matches the memory, so its Undo is refused as stale, as any later edit does today.

## Goal 4 — Deterministic tests

`SessionRecorderTests.stopReturnsBeforeExtractionFinishes` asserts a wall-clock bound under a parallel run; it should assert ordering (stop returned before the fake extraction completed) with an injected clock or a completion signal, not elapsed time. `MemoryMatcherTests.aNilEmbedderWritesNothingAndSuggestsNothing` sorts two memories created in the same instant; the fixtures get distinct `createdAt` values.

## Copy

- Status line: "Still sorting {n} moment(s) from today's recording…", "…from {weekday}'s recording…", "…from {n} recordings…", "Checking for repeats…", "Not sorting right now", "Add a key in Settings to sort what you say".
- Settings: "Your copy", "Export a copy…", "Restore from a copy…", "Replace everything on this phone with this copy?", "This copy was made by a newer version of Lifeline."
- Change sheet: "Which part of the recording", handles "Start" / "End", "Save", "Play".

## Tests the plan must carry

- Kit: `SortingWorker.start()` re-entrant; `SortingStatus.line` for every state (none, one session, older session, several, pass running, no key, network); status empties when the last segment lands; archive round trip (export → restore into a fresh directory → same counts, same audio bytes); manifest version refusal; WAL-checkpointed copy equals the source; retime within word boundaries, refused outside the segment, continuation spans untouched; the two flaky tests pass 20 times in a parallel run.
- UI: restore shows the relaunch sentence and the next launch lands on the copy; the status line appears on the timeline while the scripted sorting is slow (`-uiTesting-slow`) and disappears when it lands; export opens the share sheet; restore from a scripted archive replaces the timeline; trimming a telling moves the Play range.
