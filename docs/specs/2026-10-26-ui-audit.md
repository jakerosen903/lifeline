# UI audit after the first month of real use

Status: draft for the owner's review, 2026-10-26.

## Why

The owner used the app on their phone for about a month. They report three problems:

- Cards appear after a memory card with no link to it. "Sounds like it belongs to: family" reads as random.
- Almost everything sits in "About".
- The timeline is one unsorted block named "Outside any chapter".

This audit looks at the owner's real data and at screenshots of every main screen. It lists what is wrong, why, and what to change. It makes no code changes.

## What we looked at

- The owner's database, copied from their phone to the owner's Mac. It holds 22 sessions, 37 memories, 6 stories, 3 chapters, 30 entities and 12 domain tags. The copy stays on the owner's Mac. Nothing from it goes into the repo.
- Screenshots of the timeline, the entity lists, an entity page and the sessions list, taken in the simulator with that database loaded.
- The code that builds each screen.

All examples below are invented. They have the same shape as the owner's data.

## Findings

Severity: **High** means the screen fails its job. **Medium** means it works but feels wrong. **Low** means polish.

### Cards

**F1. Follow-up cards have no link to their memory (High).** The hint card shows only "Sounds like it belongs to: “family”". The story hint, the collection hint and the entity card have the same problem. Each one is a separate card in the queue, after the memory card the person just kept. Nothing on the card says which memory it is about. (PRINCIPLES 9: the person must never feel they are maintaining a database.)

### Timeline

**F2. Chapters are mostly empty (High).**
- Only 5 of the 37 memories are in a chapter.
- One chapter has no title and no memories, and the screen shows it as "Untitled chapter".
- A second chapter has no dates and holds a single fact.
- The third chapter takes its range from its one dated memory, so a four-year period reads as "2017–18".
- Every chapter draws its own full 2005–2025 axis, even when it is empty.

**F3. "Outside any chapter" is the timeline (High).** Nine of the eleven dated events sit in one card under this name. The name describes what is missing, not what is there. Inside the card the rows are sorted by date, but nothing groups them: a childhood house, a hurricane, a first friend and a road trip at 19 run together.

**F4. Uncertainty is a task on every row (Medium).** Most dated rows show an orange line plus "Check this: sometime between 2005–08?". Uncertainty must be shown (PRINCIPLES 5), but a question on every row turns the timeline into a to-do list (PRINCIPLES 9).

**F5. Every undated row carries two buttons (Medium).** "Add a date" and "Add to a collection" appear under every undated memory. That is the database feel PRINCIPLES 9 forbids.

**F6. The summary line shows the imbalance (Low).** "11 memories · 3 chapters · 2004 → now · and 23 things about your life".

**F7. The navigation is six unlabeled icons (Medium).** The floating bar has: hidden, tags, sessions, collections, people and settings. None has a label. The two record buttons at the bottom cover the last row.

**F8. Titles are fragments (Medium).** Examples of the shape: "an online thing", "the illness", "she was at college", all in lower case. A title must name the moment.

### About and entities

**F9. "About" holds 62% of everything (High).**
- 23 of the 37 memories are facts or feelings. The timeline shows all of them in one flat "About" list, with no grouping.
- Many are about another person. Example of the shape: "My sister and I were always close" or "He lived next door".
- Only 3 memories in the whole database have a detail link to who or what they are about.
- So the person pages are thin, and "About" collects everything else.

**F10. The owner is a person entity (High).** The extractor stored the owner's own name as a person, and linked 2 of the 3 detail links to it. Those belong on "About you".

**F11. People carry dates (Medium).** The people list shows "between 2005 and 2013" or "around 2015" under a name. That date is the span of the moments that mention the person. It reads as if the person existed only then.

**F12. Quotes show filler words and cut off mid-word (Medium).** Entity pages and the About list show the raw transcript: "we, we, -... spent a lot of time together, um, you know, a fe…". The person's words are the record (PRINCIPLES 1). Their display can be easier to read.

**F13. Entities include noise (Medium).**
- The same place appears twice with different spelling or punctuation ("Ohio." and "Ohio").
- Generic phrases are stored as places ("a restaurant", "our house").
- A casual phrase is stored as an idea.

### Stories

**F14. One story is stored three times (High, bug).** Three stories share the same title and have one moment each. Four of the six stories have only one moment. A one-moment story is just a memory with an extra row.

## Proposed direction

### Cards (owner's decision, 2026-10-26: option B)

- When the person taps Keep, the memory card stays on screen. It shows "Kept" and the first words of the memory.
- The follow-ups for that memory show on the same card as small optional chips. Examples: "Family?", "Part of a story?", "Theo — a person?".
- One tap accepts a chip. "Done", or the next memory, dismisses the rest.
- No follow-up card ever appears without its memory.

### Timeline

- Remove the per-chapter axis. Show one compact axis at the top.
- Group dated memories into life periods. Read each period from the dates and the owner's age, for example "Growing up · 2004–2012" or "College · 2015–2019".
  - A chapter the person named replaces the computed period it covers.
  - Each computed period is a guess, and is shown as one (PRINCIPLES 5).
- Do not show a chapter with no memories. Do not show a chapter titled "Untitled".
- A chapter's range comes from what the person said about the chapter, not from its first memory.
- "Outside any chapter" goes away.
- Uncertainty: each uncertain date gets a small "~" or "around". One quiet entry at the end of the timeline says "3 dates to check". Remove "Check this" from the rows.
- Undated memories: remove the per-row buttons. The actions stay in each row's ⋯ menu.

### About and entities

- A fact or feeling about a person or place shows on that page as a detail. It does not show in About.
- About keeps only what is about the owner. Group it by domain tag, for example family or school.
- The owner is never a person entity. A detail about the owner links to About you.
- The people list shows "in 8 moments", not a date range.
- Quotes on lists and pages hide filler words ("um", "uh", "like", "you know") and repeated words. The full transcript and the audio stay unchanged on the memory page (owner's call 1).
- Place names are normalized for matching: no trailing punctuation, case-insensitive. A generic place ("a restaurant") becomes a mention, not an entity, unless the person keeps it.

### Data (extractor v7)

Most of F8 to F13 starts in the extractor's output. v7 must:

- set `about_ref` on a fact or feeling whenever the segment names a person or place it is about;
- map the owner's own name and "I" to `self`;
- write a title that names the moment in sentence case, 3 to 8 words, using the person's words;
- skip generic places and casual-phrase ideas;
- keep every rule v6 has on disclosure and uncertainty.

## Owner's calls

1. **Filler words.** Hide them in quotes on lists and pages, with the full words kept on the memory page and in the audio? My recommendation: yes.
2. **Life periods.** Computed from dates and age, shown as guesses until the person names them? Or show only named chapters plus one "By year" list? My recommendation: computed periods.
3. **Existing memories.** Re-run extractor v7 over the 37 existing memories, as suggestions the person approves? Or apply v7 to new sessions only? My recommendation: suggestions, approved on a card, never applied silently (PRINCIPLES 9).
4. **Navigation.** A labeled tab bar (Timeline · People · Sessions · Settings) in place of six icons? My recommendation: yes.
