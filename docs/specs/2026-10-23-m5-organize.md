# M5 — Organize: one moment, one story, told any number of times

Status: draft for the owner's review, 2026-10-23. Follows M4.1 (PR #5, merged). Spec, not plan.

## Scope, and what this spec is not

The decisions log's "waits for M5" list has grown to two different jobs. This spec takes the first: **organizing what is kept** so that the same moment told three times is one moment, several segments of one telling are one card, related moments are one story, and the extractor sees what matters within a budget. The second job, **protecting what is kept** (the privacy screen with Share and Local-only, span-level deletion with offsets preserved, the two disabled rows on the Change sheet, in-app re-run with a new extractor version), is not part of V1: the owner is its only user (decision 2026-10-23). A plain backup and restore of the database and audio is a small task after M5.

## Why now

On the second day of M4.1 the owner had three memories of meeting their first friend. M4.1 compares a new card with one earlier memory and asks about that one; it never looks at the memories already kept. Separately, a long telling that crosses a silence boundary still becomes two or three cards, and the context packet still caps tables by count rather than choosing what is relevant.

Principles that govern this milestone: the person is the author (nothing merges, groups or is retitled without a tap); uncertainty is shown; preserve first, organize later (every action here is organization; none decides whether a telling survives; every telling keeps its audio).

## Goals

1. Memories already kept that tell the same moment are offered as a pair, "Are these the same?", and merge on one tap.
2. A retelling that matches more than one memory names them all and merges the group on one tap, with one Undo.
3. One telling that crosses segment boundaries becomes one card, not several.
4. Related moments form a story; the timeline shows stories as groups; the person titles them or approves a proposed title.
5. The context packet is chosen by relevance within a token budget; pending cards count as context.
6. A telling merged by mistake can be separated again, any time.

## Non-goals

- Life / Chapter / Year controls, "Listen at this level" playlists, pinch-to-zoom. The timeline keeps its current shape with stories as groups inside it.
- Privacy screen, span deletion, backup, re-run, eval (M6).
- Kinds and domains (later design).
- Automatic merging or grouping without a tap.

## Anti-goals

- A wrong merge that hides a distinct moment. Two trips to the same place in different years must stay two moments unless the person says otherwise. Separating later must always be possible.
- A story title the person did not write or approve. Stories are untitled until the person titles them or keeps a proposed title.
- A review chore. Pair cards arrive a few at a time in "Waiting for you", never as a queue the person must clear; nothing waits on them.

## What the person sees

### "Are these the same?" (Goal 1)

A card in Waiting for you, after the memory cards and before the questions:

> **Are these the same?**
> ▶ Met my earliest best friend Ryan · recorded Oct 23
> ▶ My 1st friend was Ryan · recorded Oct 23
> [Same story] [Keep separate]

Play on each row plays that memory's first telling. **Same story** merges the newer into the older: one memory, two tellings, the older title kept (the newer becomes the span's "also told as"). **Keep separate** records the answer so the pair is never asked again. Undo for six seconds after either. At most three pair cards show at once; more wait behind them. The card is never shown during a session or on the landing, only on the timeline.

### A card that matches more than one memory (Goal 2)

The M4.1 line becomes "Same as **Met my earliest best friend Ryan** and 1 more?" when more than one earlier memory is named. Same story merges the group: the oldest memory stays, the others fold into it as tellings, then the card's telling is added. One receipt; Undo takes the whole group back. The picker ("Which one?") lists the group first, each entry checked, so the person can untick one before merging.

### Fewer fragments (Goal 3)

A story told across a pause now arrives as one card whose quote is the first segment's and whose span covers both. Nothing visible changes except that the owner stops seeing "Right on the canal" as a separate card from "An orange house in Florida" when they were one breath apart. On the memory screen a moment that spans two segments plays through both.

### Stories (Goal 4)

On the timeline, moments the extractor said belong together appear as one story row: the story's title in serif, or when untitled, the first moment's title and "+ 2 more" in stone. Tapping expands the moments under it. A story's band on the axis spans its moments. The "…" menu on a moment gains **Move to another story…** and **Take out of its story**; on a story row, **Rename…** and **Split…** (pick the moments that leave; they form a new untitled story).

A proposed title arrives as a hint card, exactly like a collection hint: "Sounds like a story: 'Growing up with Ryan'", Keep / Not quite. Until kept, the story is untitled. The proposal is built from the person's words (the prompt's existing title rule).

Collections and chapters are unchanged: a moment can sit in its story and in a collection; a story can sit in a chapter.

### Separate a telling (Goal 6)

On the memory screen, every telling after the first has "…" → **Separate this telling**. It becomes its own memory with the telling's "also told as" title, its span, and the people and anchors that came with that card. This is how a wrong merge is undone after the six seconds.

## How it works

### Pairs (Goal 1)

- **Candidates.** For each memory (not hidden, not excluded, not a stand-in) the matcher ranks the others by cosine within the current model and keeps those in the top `pairTop = 3` with score ≥ `pairMin` (0.55 for the sentence model; no minimum for the contextual model, whose scores are not calibrated: rank only). Pairs already decided are skipped. Runs in the launch task after the backfill, and after each session lands; at most `pairBatch = 20` new pairs per run.
- **The extractor decides.** One request per batch to a new prompt `reconciler.v1.md`: the pairs' titles, quotes and range texts; strict output `[{ a, b, verdict: same_moment | same_story | different, confidence }]`. "Prefer different when unsure." Pairs it calls `same_moment` become open rows in a new table `duplicate_pair (id, a_memory_id, b_memory_id, score, verdict, status: open | merged | separate, decided_at)`; `same_story` pairs are kept with that verdict for Goal 4 (below); `different` pairs are stored `separate` so they are never asked again.
- **The person decides.** Same story → `merge(newer, into: older)` with the existing receipt and Undo; status `merged`. Keep separate → status `separate`.
- Every pair check is logged in `prompt_log` like an extraction.
- No key, or offline: nothing happens; the next run picks up.

### Groups (Goal 2)

- `MemoryMatch` grows to a list on the card: `suggestions(for:)` returns every earlier memory the extractor named (`same_as_memory_ids`, see the packet below) plus, for a calibrated model, embedding matches ≥ `suggestMin`. The line names the first and counts the rest.
- `CandidateActions.mergeGroup(_ candidate:, into memories: [MemoryID]) -> MergedCandidate`: sorts the memories by first telling, folds each later one into the oldest, then merges the card into it. One transaction per fold already exists; the receipt's `before` holds every snapshot and `unmerge` restores them all in one transaction (the M4.1 `restore(_:settingCandidate:)`).

### Reconciliation of segments (Goal 3)

- The packet carries the session's **pending memory candidates** (title, quote, candidate id), not only kept memories ("pending stories count as context", review 2026-10-22).
- The extractor's memory output gains `continues_candidate_id?: string`: this segment carries on a moment from an earlier segment of this session that is still a pending card. Prompt v5; schema change; candidate rows carry the field in their payload as always.
- When set and the earlier candidate is still pending, `ExtractionQueue` stores the new memory as a **continuation row** (kind `memory`, status `superseded`, rejection `"continues <id>"`) and **extends the earlier card**: the card's `MemoryOut` keeps its title and quote; its `segmentSpan` stays on the first segment, and a new `extra_spans` list on the payload records `(segment_id, start_word, end_word)` for each continuation. Keep creates one memory with one span per segment (the first span primary). The card stack shows the one card with its quote and "+ 1 segment" in stone.
- If the earlier card was already kept when the continuation arrives, the continuation is a normal card with `same_as_memory_id` set (the M4.1 path handles it).
- Segments still close on silence as today; nothing about recording changes.

### Stories (Goal 4)

- Tables (migration v8): `story (id, title TEXT nullable, chapter_id nullable, created_at)`, `story_moment (story_id, memory_id, "order", primary key (story_id, memory_id))`. A memory is in at most one story (unique on `memory_id`). The `chapter_id` on `memory` stays for moments outside any story; a story in a chapter puts its moments in that chapter on the timeline.
- Extractor: the packet lists the person's stories (id, title, moment titles, up to the budget); the memory output gains `story_ref?: string` (an existing story id, or a new ref shared by several memories in one output) and `story_title_hint?: string` (the person's words, max 8). The field name `same_as_memory_id` stays for "same moment"; `story_ref` is "same story, another scene". Prompt v5 says the difference in one sentence: same moment means the person is telling the same scene again; same story means another scene of the same thread.
- On Keep: `story_ref` to an existing story adds the memory to it; a new ref creates an untitled story once per session and adds each member; `story_title_hint` becomes a hint candidate of kind `story`, shown like a collection hint. Hints are accepted through the existing `acceptHint` path (`HintKind.story`).
- Pairs the reconciler called `same_story` (Goal 1) become story membership proposals: a hint card "Sounds like one story: 'Met my earliest best friend Ryan' and 'Playing sports together'", Keep / Not quite. Keep puts both in one untitled story.
- Timeline: `StoryRow (story, members: [MemoryRow], span)` inside chapters and in "On the timeline"; a story with one member renders as the moment alone. Rename, Split, Move to another story…, Take out of its story are `CandidateActions` methods with tests. Story spans are derived like collection spans. Deleting a session or a memory removes it from its story; an empty story is deleted.

### The budgeted packet (Goal 5)

- `ContextPacketBuilder` takes a `budget: Int` in tokens (default 6000; estimate by characters ÷ 4) and fills sections in this order, each by relevance until the budget is spent: previous segments (last two, as today), pending candidates of this session, related memories (matcher ranking; the current session's own memories first), people (those mentioned in the current or previous segments first, then most recently linked), stories touched by those memories, chapters, open curiosity (top by payoff), profile free text. Caps per table go; the budget rules. A section that would overflow is truncated to whole entries.
- Deterministic: the same inputs give the same bytes, as today's `packetIsStableJSON` test requires.

### Separate a telling (Goal 6)

- `CandidateActions.separateTelling(_ memory: MemoryID, order: Int) -> Memory`: a new memory with `title = span.titleText ?? memory.title`, `summaryQuote` from the span's candidate payload when it has one (else the segment text of the span), the span re-pointed at order 0, `createdFromCandidateID = span.candidateID`, people and anchors that the span's candidate named (looked up from its payload), and the source renumbered. A span with no candidate (a stand-in's first telling, a merged source's later span) separates with the segment text as quote. One transaction.

## Data changes

- v8: `story`, `story_moment`, `duplicate_pair`; `candidate.payload` carries `continues_candidate_id`, `extra_spans`, `story_ref`, `story_title_hint` (JSON, no schema change); `hint` candidates gain kind `story`.
- Prompts: `extractor.v5.md`, `reconciler.v1.md`. `ExtractorConfig.version = "v5"`. v3 and v4 decoding unchanged.

## Copy

- Pair card: "Are these the same?", rows with Play and "recorded {Mon d}", buttons "Same story" and "Keep separate"; after: "Merged. Undo" / "Kept separate. Undo".
- Group line: "Same as **{title}** and {n} more?"; picker rows checked by default for named memories.
- Story hint: "Sounds like a story: '{title}'"; membership hint: "Sounds like one story: '{a}' and '{b}'".
- Story row untitled: "{first moment title} + {n} more". Menu: "Rename…", "Split…", "Move to another story…", "Take out of its story".
- Telling menu: "Separate this telling".
- Card with continuation: "+ 1 segment" / "+ {n} segments".

## Tests the plan must carry

- Kit: pair candidates skip hidden, excluded, stand-ins and decided pairs; the reconciler's `different` never returns; Same story on a pair merges newer into older with Undo; group merge folds oldest-first with one receipt and one-transaction undo; a continuation extends the pending card and Keep makes one memory with two spans; a continuation after Keep becomes a same-as card; story tables and membership (at most one story per memory); story hint accepted creates and titles; split, rename, move, take out; an empty story is deleted on last member removal; budget fills in order and truncates to whole entries, deterministic; separate telling with and without a candidate; migration v8 on a populated v7 database; prompt v5 and reconciler prompt load; `ExtractorConfig` v5.
- UI: a pair card appears for two scripted memories the scripted reconciler calls the same, Same story leaves one row with two tellings; Keep separate removes the card and it does not return after relaunch; the scripted voice's third line continues the second and arrives as one card with "+ 1 segment"; a story row groups two scripted moments and expands; Rename a story; Separate this telling from the memory screen.
