# The Timeline: a spine, a life strip and chapters you can see across

Status: direction decided by the owner on 2026-11-16 from mock-ups. S6a (the spine), S6b (the pinned life strip and its "During" line) and S6c (chapters as names with stated years, the chapter's sheet and the life map) are built.

This spec is step 6 (S6) of the five-sections design (`docs/specs/2026-11-15-five-sections.md`, "Timeline" and "Life periods"). It replaces the chapter rules of the audit data fixes spec (`docs/specs/2026-11-16-audit-data-fixes.md`, "A1: chapters"). The owner pulled it forward of the ship order in `docs/plans/2026-11-15-sections.md`.

Mock-ups: `docs/design/TL_SpineStrip.dc.html`, `docs/design/TL_LifeMap.dc.html` and `docs/design/TL_ChapterSheet.dc.html`. The canvas with every option that was considered: https://claude.ai/artifact/PnmKtYfiQFj5DaVqgjnJm7.

## Problem

The owner reported four things after the shell, the pages and the You portrait shipped:

- Most moments sit under one heading, "Outside any chapter" (now "On the timeline"). A chapter is a box, and most moments are in no box.
- Empty chapters stay on screen. No action renames or removes one.
- The Timeline reads as a list. Rows are evenly spaced, so ten years and ten days look the same.
- A design that draws a chapter on every row breaks when there are many chapters. Chapters overlap: a home, a job, a team and a relationship run at the same time.

## Terms

- **Moment:** a memory of kind `event` or `other` with a place in time.
- **Spine:** the vertical line that the moments sit on, oldest at the top.
- **Mark:** the shape on the spine for one moment.
- **Gap:** the length of spine between two marks.
- **Life strip:** a small horizontal line of the whole life, with one tick for each moment.
- **Frame:** the box on the life strip that shows which years are on screen.
- **Chapter:** a name the person gave to a stretch of their life. It has a name. It can have stated years. It can have moments the person put in it.
- **Stated years:** the years the person said for a chapter.
- **Said member:** a moment the person put in a chapter.
- **Tag:** the chapter's name shown on a said member.
- **"During" line:** one line that names the chapters whose stated years cover the years on screen.
- **Life map:** one screen that shows every chapter as a bar on one year axis.
- **Period:** a stretch of life named by the person's age: "Childhood", "Teens", "Twenties".

## The model of a chapter

1. A chapter is not a container on the Timeline. The Timeline never groups moments under a chapter.
2. A chapter relates to a moment in two separate ways:
   - **By what the person said.** The moment is a said member. This shows as a tag.
   - **By years.** The moment's date falls in the chapter's stated years. This shows in the "During" line and on the life map.
3. The two must stay separate. A date overlap is not a claim about a moment. Example: a moment in 2001 falls in the years of "Summers at the lake", but it did not happen at the lake. The app must not tag it.
4. A moment has at most one tag. This is the stored `memory.chapter_id`. No migration is needed.
5. A chapter's stored range holds only its stated years. The span of its members is computed when the resolver needs it. It is never stored as the chapter's range and never shown as the chapter's dates.
6. A chapter that has a range from before this change, with an origin other than `stated` or `confirmed`, has no stated years.

## Design

### The screen, top to bottom

1. The title and the summary line. The summary line no longer counts chapters.
2. The pinned card: the life strip and the "During" line.
3. The spine with the moments.
4. "Not placed in time yet": one band at the end that opens the undated memories.
5. One quiet line: "3 dates to check", when there are any.

The record bar and the tabs do not change.

### The spine

1. One spine runs down the left of the list. It uses the ochre mark colour.
2. Moments show in date order, as `byTime` orders them today.
3. Each moment shows its date beside its mark, in the left margin. The words come from `TimeRange.displayText`: "2001", "around 1999", "2008 to 2010".
4. The mark shows how sure the date is. It must not use colour alone.
   - A date the person stated or confirmed: a solid dot.
   - A date that is inferred, or an "around" date: a dashed dot.
   - A range of more than one year: a dashed capsule along the spine.
5. A row shows the title, then the people and the place, then the tag. A tap on a person or place opens that page. The row keeps its "…" menu and its "in your voice" length.
6. A story with two or more moments shows as one card at its first moment, with its count, as the five-sections spec says.
7. Moments in the same year show the year once.

### Gaps

1. The gap between two marks follows the years between them. A longer time gives a longer gap.
2. The gap is compressed. It must grow with the years, and it must have a largest value.
3. From three years, the spine in the gap is dashed and carries a label: "3 years".
4. The label states a length of time only. It must not say that nothing happened, and it must not ask for a story.
5. One pure function, `SpineLayout`, computes every gap and label from the ordered dates. It stores nothing.
6. A gap is measured from the end of an earlier range to the start of the next. Ranges that overlap have the smallest gap.

### Periods

1. Periods are always computed from the birth date: "Childhood" (0 to 12), "Teens" (13 to 19), then "Twenties", "Thirties" and so on. With no birth date they are decades: "The 2000s".
2. A period shows as a waypoint on the spine, above its first moment: "Teens · by your age".
3. A period with no dated moment does not show.
4. A chapter never replaces a period. This changes the decision of 2026-10-26 and the five-sections rule "A named period is a `Chapter`". Chapters overlap, so no rule can say which chapter replaces a period.
5. The Things shelf and the Places menu keep their grouping by period. They use the computed periods.

### The pinned card

1. The card stays under the title while the list scrolls.
2. The life strip runs from the birth year to now. With no birth date it starts at the earliest moment.
3. Each dated moment is one tick. A moment with a range is a tick at the start of the range.
4. The frame covers the years of the moments on screen. It moves as the list scrolls. With Reduce Motion on, it moves without animation.
5. The "During" line names the chapters whose stated years overlap the frame's years. The longest chapter comes first. Names that do not fit become "+2".
6. With no such chapter, the card shows the strip alone.
7. A tap on the card opens the life map. The strip is not a scrubber (five-sections: "No year scrubber on the edge").
8. VoiceOver reads the card as one element: "Your life from 1998 to now. On screen: 1998 to 2003. During Maple Street, Summers at the lake and 2 more."

### Tags

1. A tag shows on a moment that is a said member of a chapter.
2. A moment becomes a said member in two ways: the person accepts the chapter on the kept card, or the person chooses "Add to a chapter…" in the moment's menu.
3. "Add to a chapter…" lists the chapters and offers a field for a new name. An empty name creates nothing. It replaces "New chapter from here".
4. A tap on a tag opens that chapter's sheet.
5. A long name truncates on one line.

### The life map

1. The life map is a sheet titled "Chapters".
2. Each chapter with stated years is one row: a bar on a shared year axis, with its name beside the bar.
3. Rows are in order of start year, then length, longest first.
4. A chapter with no stated end runs to the right edge.
5. The periods show as shaded columns with their names at the top.
6. Chapters with no stated years show under "Not placed in time yet". Each has "Say when". It opens the existing "Tell me when" sheet.
7. A tap on a row opens the chapter's sheet.
8. With no chapters, the sheet says in one sentence what a chapter is. It shows no count and no empty frame.

### The chapter's sheet

1. It shows the name, the stated years, and the said members.
2. It offers "Rename", "Say when it was" and "Remove chapter…".
3. "Remove chapter…" asks first. The memories stay; their tags go. A notice with Undo shows for 6 seconds.
4. A chapter with no name, no stated years and no said members is deleted without a question. This runs at launch and after any action that removes a member.

### What leaves the Timeline

- The section for each chapter and its year axis.
- "Outside any chapter" and "On the timeline".
- "Untitled chapter".
- The chapter count in the summary line.

## Data

- No migration.
- `chapter.range` holds stated years only (model, rule 5). `chapterRangeFromMembers`, `refreshChapterRange` and the chapter step of `ResolverRunner` must stop writing it.
- The resolver reads a chapter's stated years as an anchor target. With none, it reads the span of the said members, computed for that call.
- `story.chapter_id` stays in the schema. The Timeline no longer reads it.
- `SpineLayout` and the period function are pure functions in `Domain/`, with tests.
- The life strip reuses `LifeStrip` from the entity page.

## Edge cases

- No dated moment: the screen shows the title, the "Not placed in time yet" band and no spine, card or period.
- One dated moment: one mark, no gap, the strip with one tick.
- A moment dated before the birth date: the strip starts at that moment.
- A range that crosses two periods: the moment sits in the period of the start of its range.
- Thirty chapters over the same years: the "During" line shows what fits and "+N". The life map scrolls.
- A chapter whose stated years hold no moment: it shows on the life map. It can show in the "During" line. It has no tag anywhere.
- A said member whose date is outside its chapter's stated years: the tag shows. The app does not correct the person (PRINCIPLES 1).
- A moment that loses its date: it moves to "Not placed in time yet" and keeps its tag.
- Hidden and floating memories: unchanged.
- The largest text sizes: the date moves above the title, the margin closes, and the spine stays. The "During" line wraps to two lines before it truncates.

## Principles

- **1, the person is the author.** A tag shows only what the person said. A period is named by age only, never by a life event.
- **5, uncertainty is shown.** An uncertain date has its own mark and its own words. A chapter shows no years until the person states them.
- **9, never a database to maintain.** No empty chapter shows. No count of what is missing shows. A gap label states time and asks for nothing.

## Builder's defaults the owner can overrule

1. **One tag for each moment.** It needs no migration. More than one needs a new table.
2. **Existing chapters lose their shown years.** Their ranges came from their members. They show under "Not placed in time yet" until the owner says when.
3. **The gap label is "3 years".** The older mock-up `TL_Spine` said "quiet years", which says something about the person's life.
4. **A tap on the card opens the life map.** The strip does not scroll the list.
