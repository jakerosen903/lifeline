# M4.1 — Same story, and "when" you can answer

Status: draft for the owner's review, 2026-10-23. Follows M4 (PR #4). Spec, not plan.

## Why now

The owner's second day on the M4 build showed three things the design did not plan for:

1. A story told twice, in two recordings, becomes two memories. The extractor has a `same_as_memory_id` field, but the context packet only carries the current session's memories, and nothing acts on the field when a card is kept. Every recording from here adds duplicates the owner must clean up by hand.
2. A memory with no anchor has no way to get a date. The refine card asks the one relative question the app can compute. With no anchor there is no question, so the memory screen says "no date yet" and offers nothing. The shelf grows and the owner cannot act on it.
3. The row actions ("Add to a collection…", "New chapter from here") sit behind a "…" button the owner did not find.

The principles still hold: the person is the author; uncertainty is shown; never fill in what the person did not say. This milestone adds the moves the person can make, and a suggestion they confirm. It does not let the app merge or date anything on its own.

## Goals

- When a kept card is a retelling, the app says so, names the memory it matches, and merges on one tap. The default is still a new memory.
- A person can give any memory a date, at the precision they remember, and it displays as what they said.
- The row actions are visible without a long-press.

## Non-goals

- Stories (`story`, `story_moment`) and the token-budgeted packet stay in M5. This milestone uses retrieval to find a few related memories, not a general context budget.
- Duplicates among memories already kept (an "Are these the same?" pass and group merge) wait for M5, decided 2026-10-23 after the device run showed three tellings of one moment on the timeline.
- Automatic merging. Nothing is merged without a tap.
- Cross-person or cross-device matching.
- Free-text date parsing ("the summer before college"). Relative phrases stay with the anchor resolver.

## Anti-goals

- A wrong merge that hides a distinct moment under another one. Two drives to Disney in different years share words and must stay two memories unless the person says otherwise. A duplicate is a nuisance; a wrong merge loses a scene.
- An inferred date presented as a fact. Everything this milestone writes to a range has origin `stated`.

## What the person sees

### On the card, at Keep time

A memory card that has a likely match gains one line above its buttons:

> Same as **Orange house on Royal Palm Drive**? · recorded Oct 23

and a third button, **Same story**, beside Keep and Change…. Keep still makes a new memory. **Same story** adds this card's audio span to the named memory and shows "Added to Orange house on Royal Palm Drive" with Undo for the usual 6 seconds. The card's title is kept as an alternate title on the span, never shown as a second memory.

When the match is strong and the extractor also marked `same_as_memory_id`, the line reads the same; the app never says "this is the same". When there is more than one plausible match the line names the best one and **Same story** opens a short picker (the nearest three, newest first, with "None of these").

This applies in the session view, on the timeline's Waiting cards, and in the session detail's dismissed list when a card is brought back.

### On a memory

The Change sheet gains **Same as another memory…** under "When it was". It opens the picker above, seeded with the nearest memories, with search over titles. Choosing one merges this memory into it: spans, people, places, anchors move; collection and chapter membership move when the target has none; the merged memory keeps the target's title and range unless the target had no range and this one did. Undo for 6 seconds, then permanent.

"When it was" gains **Tell me when** under the relative question (or alone when there is no question). It offers four shapes, one picker each:

- **A year** — "1998"
- **Around a year** — "around 1998" (stored as the year with a one-year band each side, origin `stated`, granularity `year`)
- **Between two years** — "1996–99" (the app's year-span style)
- **How old I was** — "about 8" (needs the birthday; otherwise the row is disabled with "Add your birthday in Settings"). Stored as the age span the resolver already computes for age anchors, origin `stated`, evidence "about 8".

The chosen range displays as what was said, in ink, not ochre. A later relative question can still narrow it only if the person answers it; the app never overwrites a stated range.

### On the timeline rows

The "…" button stays. The row also shows its two most useful actions inline under the tags when the row is on the shelf: **Add a date** and **Add to a collection**. Placed rows show nothing extra. This is a shelf affordance; the shelf is where the owner was stuck.

## How it works

### Finding candidates: on-device sentence embeddings

- `NLEmbedding.sentenceEmbedding(for: .english)` from Apple's NaturalLanguage framework. On device, no network, no key. 512 dimensions.
- The text embedded for a memory is `title + ". " + summaryQuote`. For a card it is the card's title and quote.
- Vectors are stored in a new table `memory_embedding (memory_id PK, model TEXT, dims INT, vector BLOB)` and written when a memory is inserted or its title changes. A migration (v7) creates the table; a backfill runs at launch, in a detached task before the sorting worker, for memories with no row of the current model.
- Similarity is cosine. The kit computes it; the app only supplies the embedder.
- The embedder sits behind a protocol `SentenceEmbedder { func vector(for text: String) -> [Float]? }`. The app's implementation wraps `NLEmbedding`. Tests use a fake with hand-set vectors. On a platform where the asset is missing (`sentenceEmbedding` returns nil, which `swift test` on macOS can hit) the embedder returns nil, no row is written, and matching falls back to the extractor's `same_as_memory_id` alone. Nothing fails.

### Deciding: the extractor and the person

- Retrieval: for a segment, the nearest `k = 5` memories across all sessions with cosine ≥ `0.55` are added to the context packet's `memories`, each marked `related: true`, before the current session's memories. Excluded-from-AI memories are never retrieved. The packet stays small: five short entries.
- The extractor keeps its field. The v3 prompt line changes to: "`same_as_memory_id` names a memory from the context packet when this segment tells the same moment again or continues it; otherwise null. Prefer null when unsure."
- A card gets a match when either the extractor set `same_as_memory_id` to a packet memory, or retrieval found a memory with cosine ≥ `0.80` for the card's own text. The match carries its source so the card can log it. Thresholds are `MatchConfig` values with these defaults; they are tuned on the owner's sessions, not by hand.
- The person decides. Keep makes a new memory in all cases.

### Merging

`CandidateActions.mergeCandidate(_ candidate: Candidate, into memory: MemoryID) throws -> MemorySpan` accepts the candidate (status `accepted`), inserts a `memory_span` on the target with the next `order`, carries the candidate's people refs and place through the same linking as `acceptMemory`, and records the card title in a new `memory_span.title_text` column so the span can be labelled on the memory screen ("also told as: Grew up in an orange house"). No new `memory` row.

`CandidateActions.merge(_ memory: MemoryID, into target: MemoryID) throws` moves the memory's spans, people, places and anchors to the target, re-derives the target's range if it had none, moves collection membership when the target has none, re-embeds the target, and deletes the source row. Undo within the window restores from an in-memory snapshot; after the window the snapshot is dropped.

The memory screen lists every span under "In your voice" with its recording date and plays them in order. `SessionDetail` and the landing count a merged span as a kept moment of that session.

### Dating

`TimeRange` gains `static func stated(year:)`, `stated(around:)`, `stated(between:and:)`, and the existing age-anchor path is reused for `stated(age:birth:)`. All four set `origin = .stated` and `evidenceText` to the phrase shown. `MemoryRepository.updateRange` is unchanged. The resolver skips memories with `origin == .stated` or `.confirmed`, as it already does for confirmed.

## Data changes

- v7: `memory_embedding` table; `memory_span.title_text TEXT` and `memory_span.candidate_id TEXT`, both nullable.
- Backfill at launch: embeddings for memories without a row, cheap and idempotent.

## Copy

- Card line: "Same as **{title}**?" with "recorded {Mon d}" after a middle dot.
- Button: "Same story". After tap: "Added to {title}." with "Undo".
- Picker title: "Which one?" on a card; "Same as which memory?" from the Change sheet. Options: the nearest three, then "None of these".
- Change sheet rows: "Same as another memory…", "Tell me when".
- Date shapes: "A year", "Around a year", "Between two years", "How old I was".
- Shelf row actions: "Add a date", "Add to a collection".

## Tests the plan must carry

- Kit: cosine on fake vectors picks the nearest and respects the threshold; retrieval skips excluded memories; a card with `same_as` to a packet memory gets a match; a card with cosine 0.79 does not; `mergeCandidate` adds a span, links people, writes no memory; `merge` moves everything and deletes the source; a stated range displays in ink and the resolver leaves it alone; the embedder returning nil leaves matching to the extractor and writes no row; migration v7 on a populated v6 database.
- UI: a scripted session where the second segment retells the first shows "Same as … ?" and Same story adds a span; the memory screen shows two spans; Change → Tell me when → A year places a shelf memory on the axis in ink; the shelf row's inline "Add to a collection" works.

## Left to the builder

- Picker layout and the exact placement of the card line.
- How the backfill reports progress (probably not at all; it is seconds).
- Whether `title_text` on the span is shown on the timeline row or only on the memory screen (spec says memory screen).

## Calls made after the builder check

A reviewer read this spec as a builder and asked seven questions. The answers below are the owner's to overturn; they are written in so the plan does not guess.

1. **Delete today's recording, after a merge.** Deleting a session removes only the spans recorded in that session. A memory that keeps at least one span survives, with its spans re-ordered so the earliest telling is order 0. A memory that would lose every span is deleted, as today. **Hide today** hides the memories whose first telling (order 0) is in that session; a memory from last week that gained a span today stays visible.
2. **Match targets are kept memories only.** Retellings inside one session are already the extractor's job through the previous-segments window. The UI test keeps the first card before the second segment arrives.
3. **Stand-ins become real on merge.** A resolver stand-in ("graduation", borrowed span) is a valid target. Merging a card into it makes the card's span order 0, sets the memory's title, quote and `createdFromCandidateID` from the card, and drops the borrowed span. The stand-in's anchors and range stay.
4. **Anchors travel.** "Same story" attaches the card's anchors to the target and runs the resolver, which never overwrites a `stated` or `confirmed` range. The retelling is where the date clue usually is.
5. **Privacy of targets.** A memory excluded from AI gets no embedding row and is never retrieved or suggested; the person asked for the model to stay away from it. A memory hidden from the timeline is not suggested on a card and appears in the manual picker only when the timeline's hidden toggle is on. A span merged into a hidden memory inherits hidden.
6. **Hints follow the merge.** A collection or chapter hint on a card that went "Same story" applies to the target memory; `acceptHint` finds the memory through the span's candidate as well as `createdFromCandidateID`.
7. **Stated ranges are settled.** A `stated` range gets no further relative questions, exactly like `confirmed`. Its display text is the evidence as said ("around 1998") in ink.

## Owner's calls to confirm

1. Merge means one memory with several audio spans, as the V1 spec's `memory_span` row says. Stories in M5 can later group memories without undoing this.
2. Keep stays the default; "Same story" is the extra button, never the primary.
3. Thresholds 0.55 (retrieve) and 0.80 (suggest on the card) are starting points, tuned on the owner's recordings.
